Offer a deal

Put a deal in front of Bakersfield locals. Listing is free. Nothing goes live until Nick has talked with you.

How it works
  1. You offer a deal, like 15% off a drink.
  2. A customer follows your Instagram and @boredboardkern, then gets a 6-character code.
  3. They show the code at your counter. You tap it in to confirm.

Rate card: you pay a small flat fee only when your own cashier confirms a code. Nothing for listing, views, or codes nobody uses. Customers pay nothing. Nick tells you the exact amount before anything goes live, and you can say no.

Board Bored never sells your information.
